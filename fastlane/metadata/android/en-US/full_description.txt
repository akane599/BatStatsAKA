BatStats shows Android-reported battery readings across four tabs — Now, History, Apps and Settings — and records observed charging and discharging periods. Now shows battery level, current, voltage, temperature and supported counters with their sources and reporting times, a daily summary of today's charge and discharge, a Health card, and today's top-draining apps. Health estimates full-charge capacity from observed sessions and compares it against a design capacity; the available sysfs `charge_full` estimator is not currently used in production. Missing values remain unavailable.

A quiet, detailed monitoring notification shows observed drain and screen activity. Screen-off includes noninteractive Always On Display; CPU suspend and Android Doze are separate. Sampling cadence is fixed — 2 seconds while something needs a live reading, 30 seconds with the screen on, 300 seconds with it off — and is not a setting. A charging estimate to full uses Android's own estimate where the device supports it, otherwise a charger-taper model learned from observed charges. Battery alerts run while monitoring is active; sound and vibration are controlled in Android notification settings. On devices that report current in the wrong unit or with an inverted sign, BatStats can detect and correct this automatically from observed evidence, with a dismissible, undoable notice when it changes what's shown. The interface is dark, with an OLED (pure black) option and opt-in dynamic color on Android 12+.

Shizuku is used when running and authorized; otherwise BatStats probes root, then ADB. Non-root Shizuku needs restarting after reboot. ADB grants may persist, but on Android 16/API 36 cross-user refusal prevents ADB-only per-app batterystats access; that capability requires Shizuku or root. The Apps tab shows per-app battery/CPU/network activity from Android's own statistics, depending on device support and permissions; a Quick Settings tile shows live current/power while the shade is open. Each finished discharge session also records its own per-app breakdown automatically, taken at unplug and at the next plug-in. These use Android's statistics window, separate from local observation history. Shared UIDs limit app attribution. Activity duration alone does not prove excessive drain, and estimated mAh is not an exact energy measurement.

Browse recorded periods, inspect data sources and local diagnostics, and deliberately export or import bounded JSON/CSV history with units and timestamps. Reports stay local unless you choose to share them. Current, capacity and kernel support vary by device. Samsung hardware behavior and physical monitoring energy use remain unverified for this development build.
