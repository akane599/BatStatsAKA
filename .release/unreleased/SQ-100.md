## Changed
- Now › Today shows used and charged as a share of the battery when its full capacity is known (mAh underneath; mAh alone otherwise). A day on battery without charge-counter data reads as unavailable instead of 0.
- The session details header shows the average drain or charge rate in %/h of the battery when the capacity is known (mA underneath; mA alone otherwise), and the used/added share of the battery next to the energy.
